# Reading

Follow the reading assignments in the unit notes for scope and exercises. DDIA chapter numbers refer to the [second edition](https://dataintensive.net/).

| Unit | Main reading |
|---|---|
| Storage | DDIA Ch. 4 |
| Clocks and failure detection | DDIA Ch. 9 and [Lamport on event ordering](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/359545.359563) |
| Replication and failover | DDIA Ch. 6 and 10 and [Raft](https://raft.github.io/raft.pdf) |
| Batch and shuffle | DDIA Ch. 7 and 11, [MapReduce](https://research.google/pubs/mapreduce-simplified-data-processing-on-large-clusters/) and [Dryad](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/dryad-distributed-data-parallel-programs-from-sequential-building-blocks/) |
| Execution and planning | DDIA Ch. 3, [MonetDB/X100](https://www.cidrdb.org/cidr2005/papers/P19.pdf) and [Spark SQL](https://people.csail.mit.edu/matei/papers/2015/sigmod_spark_sql.pdf) |
| Streaming dataflow | [Streaming Systems](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/streaming-systems/9781491983867/preface01.html) Ch. 1–2 and [The Dataflow Model](https://research.google/pubs/the-dataflow-model-a-practical-approach-to-balancing-correctness-latency-and-cost-in-massive-scale-unbounded-out-of-order-data-processing/). DDIA Ch. 12 is optional background. |
| Snapshots and replay | [Chandy–Lamport](https://lamport.azurewebsites.net/pubs/chandy.pdf), *Streaming Systems* Ch. 5 and [MillWheel](https://research.google/pubs/millwheel-fault-tolerant-stream-processing-at-internet-scale/) |
| Versioned tables | DDIA Ch. 5 and 8 and [Delta Lake](https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol13/p3411-armbrust.pdf) |
| Observability | DDIA Ch. 2 and [The Tail at Scale](https://research.google/pubs/the-tail-at-scale/) |
| ML feature contracts | [Hidden Technical Debt in Machine Learning Systems](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2015/file/86df7dcfd896fcaf2674f757a2463eba-Paper.pdf) |
| ML training, actors, parallelism, and serving | [Horovod](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.05799), [PyTorch Distributed](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.15704), [Ray](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/osdi18-moritz.pdf), [Megatron-LM](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.08053), [GPipe](https://arxiv.org/abs/1811.06965), [ZeRO](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.02054), [Attention Is All You Need](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.03762) and [PagedAttention](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.06180) |
